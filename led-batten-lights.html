Why Our LED Batten Lights Perform Better

A good batten light is more than just a long LED strip in a housing. We engineer ours for real-world Indian conditions:

  • Stable, flicker-free drivers designed for fluctuating Indian voltage with surge protection.
  • Uniform, glare-controlled diffusers comfortable for kitchens, study rooms and offices.
  • Polycarbonate and aluminium body durable, lightweight and rust-resistant.
  • BIS approved and ISO 9001:2015 certified built to documented quality standards.

Sizes, Wattages & Mounting

Our linear LED batten range covers the most commonly used sizes in residential and commercial projects:

  • Lengths: 1 foot, 2 feet, 4 feet
  • Wattages: 5W, 10W, 18W, 20W, 22W, 36W, 40W
  • Colour temperatures: 3000K, 4000K, 6500K
  • Mounting: surface-mount and suspended options
  • Variants: slim batten, premium batten, IP-rated batten for utility areas
