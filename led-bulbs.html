Why Our LED Bulbs Last Longer

A bulb may look simple from the outside, but its real performance depends on engineering decisions inside:

  • Reliable LED drivers designed for Indian voltage conditions and surge protection.
  • Efficient thermal design heat-conductive housing keeps the LED cool, preserving brightness.
  • High-quality LED chips consistent colour temperature batch after batch.
  • BIS approved and ISO 9001:2015 manufactured full electrical safety compliance.

Wattages, Bases & Variants

Our LED bulb catalog covers every common home and commercial requirement:

  • Wattages: 5W, 7W, 9W, 12W, 15W, 18W, 22W, 30W and higher
  • Bases: B22 (bayonet) and E27 (screw)
  • Colour temperatures: 3000K Warm White, 4000K Natural White, 6500K Cool Daylight
  • Variants: standard household bulbs, high-wattage commercial bulbs, premium long-life bulbs
  • Beam: omnidirectional, 220-degree wide-angle coverage
