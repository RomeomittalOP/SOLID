Product Overview

The ECO Slim & Surface LED Panel from Solid State Lights is engineered for offices, retail spaces, hospitals and commercial interiors that demand uniform, glare-controlled illumination. Available in 6W, 8W, 12W, 15W, 18W, 22W wattages and Slim Round, Slim Square, Surface Round, Surface Square variants, this commercial LED panel light uses high-CRI SMD chips and surge-protected drivers designed for Indian voltage fluctuations. Slim aluminium frame construction and false-ceiling friendly mounting make installation quick, while a 50,000+ hour operational life keeps maintenance costs low. Every unit is manufactured under ISO 9001:2015 quality systems and backed by BIS-approved componentry — the reason architects and electrical contractors across Maharashtra, Sangli, Kolhapur, Pune, Thane and pan-India specify Solid State panels for demanding project requirements.
