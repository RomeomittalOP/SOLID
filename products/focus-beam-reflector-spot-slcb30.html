Focus Beam Reflector Spot — SLCB30

Focus beam · reflector · 7/12/18W · Black/White

Downlight

Product Overview

The Focus Beam Reflector Spot is a premium COB LED downlight from Solid State Lights, engineered for anti-glare comfort and long operational life. Available in 7W, 12W, 18W wattages and Black, White finishes, this recessed COB downlight combines a high-density chip-on-board LED source with a precision reflector to deliver sharp, focused light without hot-spots. Ideal for false-ceiling installations in modern homes, retail spaces, hospitality projects and corporate offices. Aluminium heat-sink body ensures thermal reliability, quality driver ensures flicker-free performance, and the 50,000+ hour lifespan reduces long-term replacement cost. BIS approved and ISO 9001:2015 manufactured — trusted by architects, contractors and dealers across India.
