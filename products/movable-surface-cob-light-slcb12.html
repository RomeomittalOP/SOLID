Product Overview

The Movable Surface COB Light is a surface-mounted COB LED light from Solid State Lights, designed for ceilings where recessed installation is not possible — concrete slabs, RCC roofs, exposed architectural ceilings and premium interiors. Available in 7W, 12W, 18W wattages and White, Black, Gold, Rose-Gold finishes. The aluminium heat-sink body with quality LED driver ensures thermal safety and flicker-free performance, while the cylindrical form factor delivers a clean, contemporary look ideal for modern homes, cafes, hotel lobbies and retail spaces. BIS approved, ISO 9001:2015 manufactured, backed by 50,000+ hour lifespan.
