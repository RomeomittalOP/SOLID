Product Overview

The LED Track Light is a premium retail track light from Solid State Lights, built for showroom, gallery and hospitality environments where flexible, adjustable lighting matters. Available in 10W, 20W, 30W wattages and Black, White finishes, each track head rotates and tilts independently for precise beam aim. Uses high-CRI COB LEDs with anti-glare reflectors so merchandise, artwork and displays appear sharp and true-to-colour. Follows industry-standard 3-wire track mounting for compatibility with existing rail systems. BIS approved, ISO 9001:2015 manufactured — supplied pan-India to retail chains, jewellery showrooms, art galleries and hospitality projects.
