Hotel Lobby & Corridor Lighting — Sangli, 2024

Hospitality / Boutique Hotel • Sangli, Maharashtra • 3,200 sqft • 2024

The Brief

A 28-room boutique hotel undergoing a 6-month refurbishment needed to replace its ageing halogen and CFL lighting across the lobby, reception, corridors and cafe. Ceiling was a mix of drop gypsum (in the lobby) and exposed RCC (in the older corridor sections). Owner wanted warm, hospitality-appropriate lighting that didn't need any maintenance in the first 3 years.

Products Supplied

QtyProduct
36 ×12W Anti-Glare COB Downlight
SLCB02, 3000K, lobby and reception drop-ceiling
48 ×12W Surface Cylinder
SLCB05, 3000K white finish, corridor RCC ceilings (no cut-out required)
14 ×18W Movable Surface COB
SLCB12, black finish, feature-wall accent in cafe

Outcome

The refurbishment completed on schedule. 18 months in, zero fixture replacements. Corridor surface cylinders handled the humid climate with no thermal issues (aluminium heat-sink construction pays off in coastal-adjacent Maharashtra). Guest reviews on Booking.com and TripAdvisor picked up +0.3 stars on average after the refurb, with lighting mentioned specifically in 4% of post-stay reviews.

“For an older building with a mix of ceiling types, having one lighting supplier who could deliver both recessed and surface fixtures was the deciding factor. And 18 months in, we haven't opened the toolkit for a single downlight replacement.” — Hotel Owner, Sangli

Key Learnings

  • Surface cylinders are the right answer for RCC/concrete ceilings — retrofit installs in 6-8 hours per corridor without any ceiling work.
  • Coastal-adjacent Maharashtra humidity is punishing for cheap driver electronics — specify aluminium heat-sink bodies and BIS-approved drivers.
  • In hospitality, warmer 3000K throughout beats mixed CCT — guests feel the difference even if they can't articulate why.
