What Makes Our Surface Cylinder Lights Different

A surface cylinder light combines the focused beam of a COB spotlight with the easy installation of a surface fixture. Our surface cylinder lights are engineered around four pillars:

  • No-cut installation — mounts directly to the ceiling surface, perfect for concrete and RCC ceilings where recessed cutting is impossible.
  • Reliable driver electronics — designed for Indian voltage conditions, with surge and overload protection for stable, flicker-free performance.
  • Aluminium thermal management — die-cast aluminium body keeps the LED cool, extending operational life and protecting lumen output.
  • Anti-glare optics — deep-set COB modules with quality reflectors for comfortable, controlled light in retail and hospitality spaces.

Surface Cylinder Lights — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a surface cylinder light?

A surface cylinder light is a surface-mounted COB LED fixture in a cylindrical housing. Unlike recessed downlights, it requires no ceiling cut-out, making it ideal for concrete ceilings and architectural installations.

Where can surface cylinder lights be installed?

They are used in modern homes, retail showrooms, restaurants, cafes, hotel lobbies and any project with exposed or concrete ceilings where no false-ceiling cut-out is possible.

What wattages are available?

Our surface cylinder lights are available in 5W, 7W, 10W, 12W, 15W and 18W with 24° and 36° beam angles in white, black, gold and rose-gold finishes.

Do surface cylinder lights run hot?

No. Our cylinder bodies use aluminium heat-sink design with quality drivers, keeping operating temperatures low for safe surface mounting and long LED lifespan.

Can I get them in custom finishes for projects?

Yes. Custom finishes and bulk quantities are available for architects, interior designers and project contractors across India.
