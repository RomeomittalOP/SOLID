What Makes Our COB Downlights Different

A COB (Chip-on-Board) LED module gives you a single concentrated light source instead of multiple dots, which means uniform illumination, controlled glare and a premium architectural look. Our COB downlights are engineered around four pillars:

  • Reliable driver electronics — designed for Indian voltage conditions, with surge and overload protection for stable, flicker-free performance.
  • Aluminium thermal management — die-cast aluminium heat sinks keep the LED cool, extending operational life and protecting lumen output.
  • True optical performance — anti-glare reflectors and consistent beam angles for offices, showrooms, restaurants and luxury interiors.
  • BIS approved & ISO 9001:2015 certified — manufactured under documented quality systems, batch after batch.

COB Downlights — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a COB LED downlight?

A COB (Chip-on-Board) LED downlight uses a single high-density LED chip cluster to produce focused, glare-controlled light. It's ideal for recessed ceiling lighting in homes, offices and retail spaces.

What wattages and beam angles are available?

Our COB downlights are available in 7W, 12W, 18W, 24W and higher wattages with 24°, 36° and 60° beam angles, in warm white (3000K), neutral white (4000K) and cool daylight (6500K).

What is the lifespan of SOLID COB Downlights?

Our COB downlights have a lifespan of 50,000+ hours under normal use, backed by quality components and BIS-approved drivers.

Are COB downlights suitable for commercial spaces?

Yes. Our anti-glare COB downlights are widely specified for retail showrooms, hotels, hospitals, offices and architectural projects across India.

Do you offer bulk supply for projects?

Yes. We offer pan India bulk supply, custom wattages and dealer pricing for contractors, architects and project consultants.
