What Makes Our COB Downlights Different

A COB (Chip-on-Board) LED module gives you a single concentrated light source instead of multiple dots, which means uniform illumination, controlled glare and a premium architectural look. Our COB downlights are engineered around four pillars:

  • Reliable driver electronics — designed for Indian voltage conditions, with surge and overload protection for stable, flicker-free performance.
  • Aluminium thermal management — die-cast aluminium heat sinks keep the LED cool, extending operational life and protecting lumen output.
  • True optical performance — anti-glare reflectors and consistent beam angles for offices, showrooms, restaurants and luxury interiors.
  • BIS approved & ISO 9001:2015 certified — manufactured under documented quality systems, batch after batch.

Wattage, Beam Angles & Colour Temperatures

Our COB Downlight India range is available across multiple wattages, beam angles and colour temperatures to match the needs of every project:

  • Wattages: 5W, 7W, 10W, 12W, 15W, 18W, 20W, 25W, 30W & higher on request
  • Beam angles: 15°, 24°, 36°, 60° for accent, task and general lighting
  • Colour temperatures: 3000K Warm White, 4000K Natural White, 6500K Cool Daylight
  • Trims: round, square, deep recessed, anti-glare, fixed and tiltable
