Why Professionals Choose Our Track Lights

Track lighting is one of the most powerful tools in retail and hospitality design. Our products are built to deliver focused light, flexible layouts and premium aesthetics.

  • Sharp, controlled beams COB modules with anti-glare reflectors highlight products cleanly.
  • Adjustable heads 350-degree rotation and 90-degree tilt for changing displays.
  • Compatible track systems single-circuit, multi-circuit and magnetic track options.
  • Reliable, BIS approved drivers for continuous retail and hospitality use.

Wattages, Beam Angles & Variants

Our track lighting catalog covers everything from accent lighting to wash lighting:

  • Wattages: 7W, 10W, 12W, 15W, 20W, 25W, 30W, 35W
  • Beam angles: 15deg, 24deg, 36deg, 60deg
  • Colour temperatures: 3000K, 4000K, 6500K (high-CRI options for retail)
  • Formats: classic 3-wire track, magnetic 48V track, linear modules, pendants
  • Finishes: matte black, premium white
