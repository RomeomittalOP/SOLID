LED Track Lights — Frequently Asked Questions

What wattages of LED track lights do you manufacture?

Our LED track lights are available in 10W, 20W, 25W, 30W, 35W and 50W with 24°, 36° and 60° beam angles, in black and white finishes.

Are SOLID track lights compatible with standard track rails?

Yes. Our COB track heads follow industry-standard 3-wire track mounting for easy replacement and expansion on existing track rails.

Where are LED track lights typically used?

LED track lights are used for retail showrooms, art galleries, museums, boutiques, jewellery stores, hospitality areas and architectural accent lighting where adjustable directional light is needed.

Can the track heads be adjusted after installation?

Yes. Each track head rotates and tilts independently, so the lighting angle can be re-aimed any time without changing the rail or fixtures.

Do you supply LED track lights for retail chain projects?

Yes. We supply LED track lights pan India for retail chains, contractors and project consultants with bulk pricing and dedicated technical support.
