Quick answers about Solid State LED indoor lighting products and services.

What types of LED indoor lighting products does Solid State offer?

Solid State offers LED panel lights, LED downlights, COB spot lights, surface lights, ceiling lights, and commercial indoor lighting solutions for residential, office, retail, hospitality, and industrial applications.

Is Solid State an LED lighting manufacturer in India?

Yes. Solid State is an Indian LED lighting manufacturer and supplier providing high-quality indoor LED lighting products for dealers, contractors, architects, builders, and project requirements across India.

Where are Solid State LED lights commonly used?

Our LED lighting solutions are widely used in offices, showrooms, hotels, hospitals, shopping malls, homes, educational institutes, commercial buildings, and modern interior spaces.

Yes. We support bulk supply requirements for commercial lighting projects, government tenders, corporate offices, retail chains, contractors, and infrastructure developments.

Are Solid State LED lighting products energy efficient?

Yes. We offer customized LED lighting options with different wattages, dimensions, beam angles, color temperatures, and application-specific configurations.
